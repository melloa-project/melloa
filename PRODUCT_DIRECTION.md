# Product direction

## The product

Melloa is the private, owner-controlled home of Melli: one persistent AI partner whose
understanding of the owner can deepen over years while models, providers, and processes change.

The north star is simple:

> The owner voluntarily chooses Melli for personal matters because she already understands the
> context and relationship.

Melli must periodically create value that a stateless assistant could not: recall why a choice was
rejected, connect events across time, notice a useful pattern, follow up on an intention, adapt after
advice fails, or decide that interrupting would not be worthwhile.

The primary interface should feel like talking with Melli, not administering an AI runtime.

## Current truth

The `v0.2.0` preview proved several technical boundaries, but it over-invested in architecture,
schemas, inspection, release evidence, operations, and fixed demonstrations before proving daily
owner value. Its fixture response is not intelligence, its memory surface is assertion
administration, and its many permanent console areas make infrastructure the product personality.

That preview is a baseline to subtract from, not a compatibility promise or roadmap.

## Current product phase: subtract

Do not add substantial new intelligence features until a post-reset baseline demonstrates all of
the following:

- active guidance tells one owner-first story and contains no milestone implementation queue;
- speculative architecture and historical release evidence are absent from the active tree;
- conversation is the overwhelmingly obvious primary experience;
- infrastructure, provider, audit, memory-record, and operations detail is contextual or advanced,
  not permanent navigation;
- the ordinary message path can be explained directly from owner words to contextual response to
  future continuity;
- code and tests retained for hypothetical future needs have been removed or explicitly justified;
- the reset is net-negative in active concepts, screens, and implementation;
- independent adversarial reviewers have inspected the repository and rendered experience.

Safe temporary gaps are preferable to retaining a polished implementation of the wrong product.
The independent Guardian boundary, owner-data control, deterministic action authorization, explicit
disclosure, and recovery-critical data integrity remain intact during subtraction.

## Next hypotheses, not an architecture roadmap

After the subtraction checkpoint, build and dogfood one complete owner experience at a time.
These hypotheses are deliberately ordered by owner value and may change when evidence disagrees.

1. **Excellent conversation and continuity.** A capable model, fast interaction, natural threads,
   and enough recent history that conversation does not mysteriously reset.
2. **Memory through benefit.** Melli forms cautious, sourced memories during normal conversation,
   uses them when relevant, and makes correction easy without asking the owner to manage records.
3. **A few real goals.** Melli understands context, priority, uncertainty, and change around a small
   number of owner goals instead of exposing a generic goal framework.
4. **Reflection that earns attention.** Periodic reasoning surfaces only meaningful change,
   unresolved commitments, patterns, or questions.
5. **One longitudinal loop.** Melli understands a goal, accumulates context, tries an intervention,
   observes the result, evaluates it, and adapts.
6. **Calibrated initiative.** Melli reaches out only when expected usefulness exceeds interruption
   cost, and learns from whether the owner was glad she did.

Do not implement these as six horizontal subsystems. Each step must arrive as a coherent owner
experience with a compelling before/after story.

The intended proactive shape is goal-first and event-bounded: the owner sets a goal with Melli; a
small shared goal workspace records current intent, constraints, evidence, questions, interventions,
and outcomes; deterministic connectors ingest agreed facts from sources such as calendar, email
metadata, Strava, Apple Health or Workout exports, task completions, webhooks, or files; scheduled
jobs aggregate state; model calls happen at judgment points; and external effects remain narrow,
inspectable, and owner-authorized. Melli may propose connector, webhook, scheduler, datastore, or
dashboard code through the bounded self-change path, but deployed code runs under deterministic
capability and provenance checks. This is not ambient autonomy. It should follow the first
recoverable Telegram/self-change server proof, not replace it.

## Product evidence

Automated tests remain important for deterministic behavior and hard boundaries. They do not prove
that Melli is worth using.

Primary product evidence comes from sustained use with real conversations, goals, corrections,
changed circumstances, failed suggestions, and useful surprises. Capture failures in owner language:

- Why did Melli forget this?
- Why is she asking again?
- Why did she interrupt me?
- Why did she miss this connection?
- Why is this technically correct but useless?
- What became valuable specifically because she knew the history?

Judge major work by owner time saved, cognitive load removed, insight created, follow-through, and
whether trust increased. Configuration activity, dashboard completeness, and fixture success are not
proxies for those outcomes.

## Deliberate deferrals

Until real dogfooding requires them, defer cameras, broad plugin ecosystems, generic multi-agent
frameworks, provider matrices, arbitrary self-modification, production-scale distributed
infrastructure, complex hardware, marketplace concepts, and speculative schemas.

At every major decision ask: if the owner had used Melli for two years, would this help her earn a
place in that person's life?
